# Finding the right book and the right page

## R-BOOK-001

when: looking for the register of a village
do: search the village name in the description of the volume, not only the parish name; a small village belonged to a neighbouring parish and a filial village could have its own register even if the certificate came from the mother parish
why: the parish named in a later record is not always the parish that kept the register of the village
source: reference/matriky.md#Jak vypadá hledání v praxi

## R-BOOK-002

when: an index gives a folio or page number
do: never convert it to an image number with a fixed offset; read the page number on two or three images, calculate the shift and only then jump (the shift changes inside a book because of inserted or missing leaves)
why: assumed offsets sent the search to the wrong place by many images
source: reference/matriky.md#Jak vypadá hledání v praxi

## R-BOOK-003

when: a marriage is not found
do: look first in the bride's parish (usually her place of residence), then in the groom's; if the register under the bride's surname shows nothing, try the groom's surname because indexes differ in arrangement
why: the marriage usually took place where the bride lived
source: reference/matriky.md#Jak vypadá hledání v praxi

## R-BOOK-004

when: a family is missing in the Catholic register
do: try the Protestant register (after the Toleration Patent) and the Jewish register; for the 20th century also the civil registers of persons without confession; remember the mixed-marriage rule (from 1868 sons followed the father and daughters the mother, before 1868 all children of a Catholic father were Catholic)
why: the family belonged to a different confession and its records are in a different series
source: reference/matriky.md#Jak vypadá hledání v praxi
