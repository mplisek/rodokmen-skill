# Negative results ("not found")

## R-NEG-001

when: a search in an index finds nothing
do: treat it as no evidence; the index is incomplete and may be arranged differently than expected; record exactly what was searched (books, years, images) and read the record itself before concluding
why: "not in the index" was recorded as "does not exist" and the record was found later on a page the index skipped
source: reference/matriky.md#Typické pasti (ze skutečného výzkumu)

## R-NEG-002

when: about to write "not found" for a parish or a book
do: write it only for a book that really covers the village; check the field listing the localities of the volume and look for a separate book of the village
why: a wedding was "not found" in two parishes while it was in the village book of the correct parish
source: reference/matriky.md#Knihy řazené po vsích (časté 1784–1900, všechny archivy)

## R-NEG-003

when: a book is arranged by villages (each village has its own section and page numbering)
do: map all sections of the book before concluding; the section of a village is often at the end of the book, so estimating the image from the year fails
why: only one section was searched and the wedding was in the section of the market town
source: reference/matriky.md#Knihy řazené po vsích (časté 1784–1900, všechny archivy)

## R-NEG-004

when: reading an alphabetical index
do: cut the full-resolution image into vertical strips and read each column separately, line by line; record a negative result from an index only after reading it this way; also check the letters under which a scribe could have filed the name (St for Š, W for V, C and K, G and K)
why: reading a double page at once overlooks a name; one "not in the index" was wrong three times and the family was then found three generations further
source: reference/matriky.md#Čtení písma
