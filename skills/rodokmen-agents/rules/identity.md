# Identification of persons

## R-ID-001

when: two persons of the same name appear in a parish and you are about to treat them as one
do: merge them only on two independent signs (house number, both parents, spouse, the same circle of sponsors, an exact birth date), each sign from a different record written by a different hand; one sign or only name and period means `prob` with a `note` saying why, and the conflict goes to the open issues
why: two men of the same name were merged into one, and the children of "that" couple were born ten months apart
source: reference/metodika.md#Ztotožnění – kdy je to tatáž osoba

## R-ID-002

when: cousins of the same name live in one village
do: do not rely on the house number alone (they lived next to each other or in one house); require the parents and the wife
why: the house number identifies a household, not a person
source: reference/metodika.md#Ztotožnění – kdy je to tatáž osoba

## R-ID-003

when: about to record "the same person"
do: list the other candidates of the same name in the parish and for each say why it does not fit; state which new evidence would refute the conclusion
why: a conclusion without the rejected alternatives cannot be checked later
source: reference/metodika.md#Ztotožnění – kdy je to tatáž osoba

## R-ID-004

when: a widow who remarried appears under two surnames after her husbands
do: match her by the maiden surname and the father's name in both marriage records
why: the surname changes with each marriage, the father's name does not
source: reference/metodika.md#Ztotožnění – kdy je to tatáž osoba

## R-ID-005

when: sources contradict each other
do: prefer the original over a copy; weigh who reported the fact and how long after the event (a birth date in a death record reported by a neighbour is weaker than the baptism record); record the contradiction in the open issues
why: a silently chosen value hides the doubt from everyone who reads the data afterwards
source: reference/metodika.md#Ztotožnění – kdy je to tatáž osoba
